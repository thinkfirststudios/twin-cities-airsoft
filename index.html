04First time?

New here? Start here

Five steps. No jargon.

  1. Check the age rules

    Must be 10+. Ages 10–17 are required to wear a made-for-paintball full face and goggles system — “This is an insurance rule.”

  2. Get the waiver signed before you drive

    Under 18 must have a waiver signed by a parent. Waiver details.

  3. Bring bio BBs only

    BIO BB ONLY course.

  4. Have the two required items

    A barrel cover ($8) and a red dead rag, 12" × 12" ($5) — both required, both on the published price list.

  5. Travel with the gun out of sight

    Transport guns out of sight — gun case or trunk.

05Rules & chrono

Know your numbers

Three headline limits. Each one only applies with the condition printed under it — read the full table before you build.

Full-auto cap
30RPS

Full-auto capped at 30 Rounds Per Second.

Non-sniper // AEG, HPA/GBBR, DMR
317FPS

Less than 1.5 joules, on FIELD-PROVIDED .32g BBs — equivalent to 400 FPS with .20g. HPA / GBB: same 317 FPS.

Snipers only // bolt-action
394FPS

Max with field .32g. Bolt-action only, 100 foot minimum engagement distance; inside 100ft, switch to a backup in standard chrono range.
