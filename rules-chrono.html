Chrono rules

The published chrono figures are measured with field-provided .32g BBs. Limits are stated per class — a limit for one class does not apply to another.

Chrono rules — as published by Twin Cities Airsoft
ClassRule
Full-autoCapped at 30 Rounds Per Second.
Non-sniper (AEG, HPA/GBBR, DMR)Must chrono at 317 FPS (less than 1.5 joules) using FIELD-PROVIDED .32g BBs — equivalent to 400 FPS with .20g.
HPA / GBBSame 317 FPS limit.
SnipersBolt-action only · 100 foot minimum engagement distance · must not exceed 394 FPS with field .32g · to shoot inside 100ft, a sniper must switch to a backup in standard chrono range.
EnforcementAn unmarked or unchecked gun = banned from all future events, no exceptions.

Read the sniper limit carefully

The 394 FPS figure applies to bolt-action snipers only, with a 100 foot minimum engagement distance. It is not a general limit. To shoot inside 100ft, a sniper must switch to a backup in standard chrono range.

Marking & enforcement

An unmarked or unchecked gun = banned from all future events, no exceptions.

Age policy

  • Must be 10+.
  • Ages 10–17 are required to wear a made-for-paintball full face and goggles system — “This is an insurance rule.”
  • Under 18 must have a waiver signed by a parent.

Staging area

  • No firing, ever.
  • No magazines in guns, ever.
  • An actual (not homemade) barrel blocking device is required — sleeves, plugs or caps only. Never gloves, socks or a hand.
  • Pistols may be holstered.

BB type

BIO BB ONLY course.

Transport

Transport guns out of sight — gun case or trunk.

Required gear

  • Barrel cover — $8 — required.
  • Red dead rag, 12" × 12" — $5 — required.

The full rental package includes goggles with full facemask. Ages 10–17: see the age policy above.

Waiver

Everyone plays on a signed waiver. Under 18 must have a waiver signed by a parent.
